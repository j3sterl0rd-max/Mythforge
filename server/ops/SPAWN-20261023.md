# New Overworld spawn

The enclosure is in `minecraft:overworld`, centered on X=0, Z=0. The old beta dimension (`mythforge:old_overworld`) and the Refuge dimension are not construction targets.

- Outside footprint: 201 x 201 blocks, X/Z -100 through 100. This matches the measured non-air footprint of the Refuge island templates.
- Bedrock: outside faces, floor Y=-64 and ceiling Y=319, one block thick.
- Black concrete: the immediately adjacent inner faces, floor Y=-63 and ceiling Y=318, one block thick.
- Usable empty interior: X/Z -98 through 98, Y=-62 through 317, except the single glowstone at (0,127,0).
- Shared world spawn: (0,128,0), with spawnRadius=0.

`kubejs/server_scripts/mythforge_spawn_protection.js` reads `config/mythforge-spawn.json`. Moderator, Administrator and Owner may edit blocks; normal and beta/supporter ranks do not grant building permission. Container/block use and entity interactions remain available. Item-based block edits, automated placement, piston incursions, explosion block damage and hostile spawning are blocked in the exact box. Explosions can still affect entities.

The arrival handler places first-time players and roof-spawned arrivals on the central glowstone. Existing players elsewhere retain their position.

Vanilla `spawn-protection` is set to 0 in `server.properties`, because its OP-only center protection would otherwise prevent non-OP staff building and container use. The authorized restart on October 23 activated this setting, confirmed by the runtime checks. The regional script can reload without restarting.

## Recovery and evidence

Pre-construction backups and their hashes are in `ops/audit/spawn-20261023/before/manifest.json`. Automatic saves were disabled only for the flushed backup copy and then enabled again. The backups include all four intersecting Overworld region/entity/POI files that existed, plus level data and server properties.

`ops/audit/spawn-20261023/build-spawn.py` is a resumable console builder with a per-chunk checkpoint. It validates dimension and bounds on every fill. Temporary construction tickets were removed after completion. One vanilla forced chunk at [0,0] anchors the center; no general claim/force-load allowances were increased.

`ops/audit/spawn-20261023/verify-blocks.py` checks every saved block in the enclosure, its center glowstone and the saved shared spawn. Its result is `block-verification.json` in the same folder.

All 15,513,984 saved blocks passed verification. Protection callback tests were first run in the isolated server copy; all 14 production checks passed after restart, including the disabled vanilla radius and exclusion of the old beta dimension. Results are saved under `ops/evidence/spawn-protection-tests.json`, with a publication copy under `ops/audit/spawn-20261023/protection-tests.json`. These are automated callback checks, not a joined-client interaction test.

Restoring entire region files can also revert unrelated blocks in those regions. Stop the server and restore only the affected chunks if a selective rollback is required; keep the pre-construction backup intact.
